Cups and African Football

The league is only part of the season. Mzansi Manager includes domestic cups, a continental competition and international call-ups that add trophies and extra challenges.

The Thatbank Cup

The Thatbank Cup is open to every club in the country, from the Premier League down to the 5th Division. It is a knockout competition, so a single bad day ends your run, and it is the best chance a lower-division club has of a famous upset. Cup ties that finish level are settled on penalties.

Mzansi8 and the Bayakhala Knockout

The strongest clubs also compete in the Mzansi8 and the Bayakhala Knockout. They are extra trophies to win, and they mean extra fixtures, so squad depth becomes important for clubs competing on several fronts.

The African Champions League

The best clubs qualify for the African Champions League, where they face the strongest teams on the continent. Qualification adds prestige and prize money, but also more matches and travel, which can wear down a small squad.

African Nations Cup call-ups

Your best South African players can be called up for African Nations Cup qualifiers and finals. Expect to lose key players for a period, and plan your squad so you can cope without them.

How to approach a knockout tie

A league season gives you time to recover from a bad result. A cup tie does not. The same match can be won or lost on one mistake, so think about what the tie means for your club before you pick your team.

  • Against a much weaker side: rest some key players and give academy or fringe players a start. You should still win, and your best players stay fresh for the league.
  • Against an equal side: pick your strongest team and keep tactics balanced. Extra caution costs little in a one-off match.
  • Against a stronger side: an underdog does well by defending and staying in the game. A defensive mentality gives fewer chances at both ends, and a low block keeps your shape. If the match goes to penalties, anything can happen.

The live match page explains the settings you can change mid-match, and the tactics guide covers mentality, pressing and tempo in detail.

Penalties

When a cup tie finishes level, it is settled on penalties. You cannot fully control the outcome, but you can avoid giving yourself an extra problem: make sure your substitutes are fresh and that you have used your changes sensibly before the end of extra time.

Managing a busy schedule

  • Rotate your squad so key players are fresh for the biggest games.
  • Pick the competitions that matter most to your club and the board.
  • Use cup games to give academy players valuable experience.

Planning for call-ups

Losing a star for an international period can hurt, but it hurts less if you plan. Keep at least one capable replacement in every position, and prefer players who can cover more than one role. Do not build a team that depends on a single player. The squad rotation guide shows how to keep a deeper squad fit and happy.

A season plan for a club in several competitions

  1. Decide before the season which competition matters most. For most clubs it is the league.
  2. Build a squad of 22 to 26 players so that you can rotate without losing quality.
  3. Use early cup rounds to rest tired players and test younger ones.
  4. Strengthen in January if injuries have hurt a position.
  5. Protect your key players before the biggest knockout ties and the league run-in.

Frequently asked questions

Is it worth winning the Thatbank Cup?

It is an extra trophy, it adds prize money and it helps your reputation. For a small club it is also the best chance of a memorable run, as the cup includes every division.

Can a 5th Division club win the cup?

The cup is open to every club, including those in the 5th Division, and a knockout competition always carries the chance of an upset. It is difficult, but it is part of what makes the cup special.

Do more competitions mean more money?

Prize money and cup runs can improve a season's finances, but they also bring extra matches and wear. A club with a thin squad may gain little from them.
