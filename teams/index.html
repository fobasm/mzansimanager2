Choose by division

  • Mzansi Premier League: 20 clubs. Best for managers who want trophies straight away. Deeper squads, bigger budgets, less room for error.
  • Mzansi Championship: 20 clubs. One step from the top. Competitive, with promotion places to fight for.
  • Mzansi 3rd Division: 20 clubs. The middle of the pyramid. Modest budgets, so every signing matters.
  • Mzansi 4th Division: 20 clubs. Tight money and thin squads. Free agents and the academy matter most.
  • Mzansi 5th Division: 20 clubs. The hardest road, and where every custom club begins.

Each division page lists every club with its home city: Premier League, Championship, 3rd Division, 4th Division, 5th Division.

Choose by the challenge you want

  • I want to win things. Take a Premier League club. You will have the strongest squad and a real chance at the league, the cups and the CAF Champions League, but a bad run costs you quickly.
  • I want a realistic climb. Pick a club in the 3rd or 4th Division. The budget is small enough that decisions matter and promotion is a genuine achievement. The promotion guide was written for this.
  • I want to build from nothing. Found your own club. It always starts in the 5th Division with a custom badge and your colours, and nothing is handed to you. Read Starting from the 5th Division first.
  • I want my home team. Look up your city below. Seventy-one cities and towns are represented, so there is a good chance one is near you.

Creating your own club

On the team selection screen you can design a badge, pick your colours and name the club. Custom clubs start in the Mzansi 5th Division. It is the slowest way to the top and the most satisfying, because every promotion is yours.

What to check before you commit

  • Open the Squad page and look for the weakest starting position. That is your first signing.
  • Look at the average age of the squad. An old squad wins now and declines fast.
  • Check the finances and the sponsor offers. They decide how much you can spend in the first window.

You can start again at any time with a different club, and the game has three save slots, so trying several is easy.
