Mzansi 3rd Division

The Mzansi 3rd Division is the middle of the pyramid. It is full of clubs from towns and townships across South Africa, and it is often decided by who handles the pressure of a long season best.

What it is like to manage here

This is where the game starts to open up. Budgets are modest, so every signing counts, but a smart manager can build a squad that rises two divisions in two seasons. The promotion playoffs often decide the last place in the division above, which makes the race for third to sixth place one of the most exciting parts of the season.

Promotion and relegation

The top two clubs are promoted to the Mzansi Championship, and in some seasons a playoff decides the last promotion place. The bottom two clubs are relegated to the Mzansi 4th Division. Every division has twenty clubs and a 38-match league season, with cups and other competitions played around it.

Local rivalries

Several places have more than one club in this division, which creates local rivalries:

  • Durban: Ulundi FC, FC Bayview

Manager tips for the 3rd Division

  • Watch contract expiry dates. Losing a key player for nothing is costly at this level.
  • Prioritise ratings in the positions that matter most for your formation instead of spreading your budget thinly.
  • A strong home record wins promotion races. Make sure your tactics suit your players, not just the opposition.
  • If you finish in the playoff places, rest key players before the semi-finals where possible.
