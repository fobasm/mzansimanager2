How to Play a Live Match

On matchday you can watch your team play live on a pitch. This guide explains the controls, the keyboard shortcuts for computer players, what you can do at half-time and how to read what is happening so you can make better decisions.

Live matches or instant results

When a match day arrives, the Continue button becomes Matchday. Pressing Play matchday takes you to the pre-match screens and then to the live pitch. If you prefer speed, you can switch off live mode in the Tactics page, and the game will simulate your matches instantly. Both modes use the same match engine, so you do not lose anything by simulating. Live mode is simply more engaging, and it lets you make changes while the game is in progress.

Before kick-off

Before a live match, you see a match preview with information about the opponent and then you can choose your lineup and tactics. Take a minute to check three things: that your best available players are starting, that no key player is tired or carrying an injury, and that your tactics suit the opponent. A strong side playing at home can go on the attack, whereas a weaker side away from home may want to defend deep.

The live pitch

During the match you see the players moving on the pitch, the score, the minute and a commentary feed that records goals, chances, cards and other events. Goals are celebrated with a sound and a highlight. The commentary is useful because it tells you what has happened even when you were not looking at the pitch, so scan it every few minutes.

Match speed

You can run the match at four speeds. The slowest lets you follow every move, and the faster speeds move through quiet spells quickly. Many players start at normal speed and increase it during the first half, then slow down again when something important happens. You can also pause the match at any time to think about your next move.

Keyboard shortcuts (computer)

  • Space: start or pause the match.
  • Enter: start the match, or finish when the full-time screen appears.
  • 1, 2, 3, 4: choose the match speed from slowest to fastest.
  • S: skip to the end of the match.
  • B: open the substitutions screen.

Shortcuts are available on a computer keyboard. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.

Half-time

At half-time the players walk off and the changes screen opens. This is your main chance to affect the match. You can make up to five substitutions across the match and adjust your tactics. Ask yourself a few questions. Are you losing and need more attack? Change mentality to attacking and consider a more attacking formation. Are you ahead and want to protect the lead? Move to a balanced or defensive shape and bring on fresh defenders. Is a player on a booking? A substitution can prevent a red card, which would leave you with ten men and a suspension for the next game.

Substitutions

You have five substitutions. Use them. A fresh player in the final thirty minutes is usually better than a tired starter, particularly if you are pressing high, because a high press drains the legs after about an hour. Replace players in the same position when you are happy with your shape, or change the shape by bringing on an extra attacker or defender. Substitutes who come on also get some playing time, which helps keep fringe players content.

Reading the game

Signs that you need to change something:

  • Your opponent has had several chances and you have had none. Drop deeper or switch to a defensive mentality.
  • You are dominating but not scoring. Move to a more attacking mentality, add a forward or a playmaker.
  • Two or more of your players are visibly tired. Substitute early.
  • You have been shown a red card. Switch to a defensive shape, because a team with ten men cannot hold a high press.

Full time

When the match ends, a full-time card shows the result, and the game updates the league table and other results for the round. You can review the highlights, player ratings and the player of the match. Then press Continue to move on to the next day.

Cup matches

Cup ties are played in the same way as league games, but there must be a winner. If the score is level after the match, the tie is settled on penalties, so a draw will not end the competition for you. For important cup ties, keep substitutions in reserve in case the game goes the distance.
