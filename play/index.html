Mzansi Manager 2026: the free South African football management game

Mzansi Manager is a free football manager game set in South Africa. Take charge of one of 100 clubs across five divisions, from the Mzansi Premier League down to the 5th Division, or found your own club and build it from the bottom. Pick your squad, set your tactics, watch your matches live, work the transfer market, develop young players in your academy and chase cups and continental football.

It runs in your browser on a phone or a computer, needs no download or account, and saves your career automatically.

How to play · Leagues and clubs · Strategy guides · About

Five divisions, 100 South African clubs, full squads with faces, ratings and leagues. You don't kick the ball — you pick the team, watch the match unfold, work the market and build a club over seasons.
