Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information Mzansi Manager (“we”, “us”, the website at mzansimanager.co.za) handles when you visit the site or play the game. We aim to comply with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws.

1. Information we do not collect

You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your name, email address or payment details to play the game, and we do not run a server that stores your career.

2. Game saves and local storage

Your careers, settings and preferences are stored in your own browser using local storage on your device. We cannot see them and cannot recover them if you clear your browser data or switch devices.

3. Google Sign-In (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the game uses your Google name to personalise the game in your browser. We do not store it on a server.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages and features are used. Google Analytics may set cookies and collect technical information such as your approximate location, device type, browser and pages viewed. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally to us. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements on some pages of this site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your browser settings also let you block or delete cookies. Blocking cookies will not stop you from playing the game.

6. Fonts and third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and may link to social platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and X. These services have their own privacy policies and may collect information such as your IP address when your browser loads their content.

7. Photos and emails you send us

If you email us, we use your message only to reply and, where you ask to be added to the game, to create your in-game player. Photos sent for this purpose are used only to create your in-game face and are deleted afterwards.

8. Children

Mzansi Manager is a general-audience sports game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Children should ask a parent or guardian before using sites that show personalised advertising.

9. Your rights

You may ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its use. Because we hold very little personal information, most requests can be answered quickly. Contact us at mzansimanager@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game and site change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: mzansimanager@gmail.com.
