Contact Mzansi Manager

We read every message. Whether you found a bug, have an idea, want to partner with us or want to appear in the game as a player, we would like to hear from you.

Email

mzansimanager@gmail.com

We usually reply within a few working days.

What to write to us about

  • Bug reports. Tell us what you were doing, which device and browser you use, and what went wrong. A screenshot helps a lot.
  • Feedback and ideas. Features you would like, clubs or leagues to add, balance changes.
  • Get added to the game. See the How to play page for what to include.
  • Club badge and name corrections. If you represent a club and want a correction, write to us.
  • Business and advertising. Sponsorship or partnership enquiries are welcome.
