Formations Explained

A formation is the shape your team plays in, written as defenders, midfielders and forwards. Mzansi Manager has fourteen. This page explains what each one is for, so you can pick the one that suits your squad.

Try a formation in the game

FormationShapeWhen to use it
4-4-2A flat back four, a flat midfield four and two strikers.Balanced and easy to understand. Two strikers give each other support and the wide midfielders cover both flanks. A good first choice.
4-3-3Back four, three in midfield and a front three.Strong going forward and good for stronger sides. It needs a midfielder who can protect the defence.
4-2-3-1Back four, two holding midfielders, three behind a lone striker.Solid and flexible. The two holders protect the defence while the front four create chances.
4-1-2-1-2A diamond midfield behind two strikers.Strong through the middle but thin on the wings. Needs full-backs who can get forward.
3-5-2Three centre-backs, five in midfield, two strikers.Controls the middle of the pitch. The wide midfielders do a lot of running, so stamina matters.
5-3-2Five at the back with three midfielders and two strikers.Hard to break down. A good choice for an underdog protecting a result.
4-2-3-1 (CAM)A 4-2-3-1 with wingers and a central attacking midfielder.More creative and more attacking than the standard 4-2-3-1. Rewards good wingers.
4-3-3 (Wide)A 4-3-3 with true wingers.Stretches the opposition with width. Best when you have pace on both flanks.
4-3-1-2Three midfielders, an attacking midfielder and two strikers.A narrow, central shape that feeds two strikers.
3-4-3Three at the back, four in midfield and three forwards.All-out attack. Risky if your centre-backs are slow.
3-4-1-2Three centre-backs, four midfielders, a playmaker and two strikers.A compact attacking shape with a creative player behind the strikers.
3-5-2 (WB)A 3-5-2 with wing-backs.Width from the wing-backs without losing numbers in midfield. Demanding on fitness.
5-3-2 (WB)A five-man defence with wing-backs.Defensive when needed and quick to counter when the wing-backs push forward.
5-4-1Five defenders, four midfielders and one striker.The most defensive set-up. Best for protecting a lead or surviving against a much stronger side.

How to choose

  • Start from your players. If your best players are wingers, pick a formation with wingers. If you have three strong centre-backs, a back three makes sense.
  • Match the formation to the opponent. Against a stronger side, a five-man defence helps you stay in the game. Against a weaker side, add a forward.
  • Check positions in the Squad page. A player does best in his own position, so a formation that leaves a player out of place costs you rating points.
  • Pair it with tactics. A formation is the shape and tactics are the instructions. Read the tactics guide and live matches.
