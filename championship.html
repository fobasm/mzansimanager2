Mzansi Championship

The Mzansi Championship is the second tier, a competitive division full of ambitious clubs and fallen giants all fighting for the two promotion places. It is where most managers begin their serious climb towards the top flight.

What it is like to manage here

The Championship rewards consistency. Squads are strong enough that no match is easy, but there is room for a well-run club to outperform bigger names by developing young players and spending carefully. The pressure from the board is real, because promotion is the target for most clubs, yet relegation to the 3rd Division is a constant threat for the clubs in the bottom places.

Promotion and relegation

The top two clubs are promoted to the Mzansi Premier League, and in some seasons a playoff decides the last promotion place. The bottom two clubs are relegated to the Mzansi 3rd Division. Every division has twenty clubs and a 38-match league season, with cups and other competitions played around it.

Local rivalries

Several places have more than one club in this division, which creates local rivalries:

  • Cape Town: Cape Town FC, FC Highbury, Umoya FC
  • Limpopo: FC Moletsi, Bethanie FC
  • Upington: Upington FC, New Upington FC

Manager tips for the Championship

  • Aim to be in the top six all season. In some seasons the final promotion place is decided by playoffs, so finishing high keeps you in the race.
  • Build around two or three standout players and fill the rest of the squad with reliable, affordable options.
  • Use the academy. A steady supply of promoted youngsters keeps wages down and gives you assets to sell.
  • Be careful with the January window. A good loan or free signing can transform a season, but panic buying rarely does.
