About Mzansi Manager

Mzansi Manager is a free football management game that puts African football first. Manage a club in South Africa, England or Spain, pick the team, set the tactics, work the transfer market and try to climb from the bottom of the pyramid to the top flight and, in South Africa, the CAF Champions League.

Why we built it

Football management games are among the most popular sports games in the world, but almost all of them are built around European leagues, and African football rarely gets the same attention. Mzansi Manager was built to change that. It is a football sim that puts African football first, starting with South Africa's township derbies, fierce lower-division rivalries and passionate supporters, and it is made in South Africa for people who want to see African clubs, cities and culture in a game they can play on a phone or a computer.

What you can do in the game

  • Climb the divisions. Choose from nine divisions: five in South Africa, from the Mzansi 5th Division up to the Mzansi Premier League, two in England (the English Super League and the Second League of England) and two in Spain (the Spanish Premier League and the Spanish Championship). Two clubs go up and two come down between divisions every season.
  • Watch live matches. Matches play out on a pitch in real time. You can change tactics, make substitutions at half-time and speed up or skip the action.
  • Run the transfer market. Bid for players, sign free agents, sell squad members and renew contracts, with transfer windows that follow the real calendar.
  • Build an academy. Upgrade your youth setup and promote young players into the first team.
  • Chase cups and continental football. Compete in the Thatbank Cup, the Mzansi8 and the Bayakhala Knockout, and qualify for the CAF Champions League and Africa Cup of Nations call-ups in South African careers, chase the Cup of England or the Copa de Fútbol in Spain, and take on the European Kings Cup and the Euro Contender Cup with the best English and Spanish clubs. All three countries share one world: you can follow every league from any career, browse every club, and move between countries as a manager. Overseas players cost more and earn higher wages.
  • Create your own club. Design a badge, choose your colours and found a brand-new club at the bottom of the pyramid.
  • Manage the finances. Balance wages, sponsors and budgets while keeping the board happy.

Fictional players, real flavour

Every player in Mzansi Manager is fictional. Names are generated from South African, English, Spanish and wider regional name pools, so the squads feel familiar without representing real people. The game is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Premier Soccer League, SAFA, CAF, the Premier League, the FA or any club. If you would like to appear in the game yourself as a player, see the How to play page for how to get added.

Free, and in your browser

Mzansi Manager is free to play and runs entirely in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install and you do not need an account. Your careers are saved in your own browser, across three save slots. We may show advertising on our information pages to cover the cost of running the site; see our Privacy Policy for details.
