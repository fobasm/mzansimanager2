Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Mzansi Manager. If yours is not here, contact us.

Is Mzansi Manager free?

Yes. The game is free to play in your browser. We may show advertising on our information pages to cover the cost of running the site.

Do I need an account or to download anything?

No. Open the game in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer and start playing. Signing in with Google is optional and only helps the game remember your name in that browser.

Where are my saves stored?

In your own browser, in three save slots. They are not uploaded to a server, so clearing your browser data, or switching browser or device, means the saves will not follow you. Avoid clearing site data if you want to keep a career.

Are the players real?

No. Every player is fictional. The game is not affiliated with or endorsed by the PSL, SAFA, CAF or any club.

How many clubs and divisions are there?

There are 100 clubs in five divisions of twenty: the Mzansi Premier League, Championship, 3rd Division, 4th Division and 5th Division. Two clubs go up and two go down between divisions each season.

Can I create my own club?

Yes. You can design a badge, choose colours and name the club. Custom clubs start in the Mzansi 5th Division.

Can I watch matches or skip them?

Both. Live matches show a 2D pitch where you can pause, change tactics and make up to five substitutions. You can also turn live matches off or skip to the result. See the live matches page for details.

Is there a 3D match view?

Not yet. It is planned but has no release date. The 2D live view is available now.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It runs in a mobile browser. Landscape orientation gives you the most room. We are continuing to improve the phone layout.

How do I get promoted?

Finish in the top two of your division. Our promotion guide covers squad audits, budget, tactics and form in detail.

How do I appear in the game as a player?

Send us a message through the contact page and we will tell you how to get added.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Use the contact page. Tell us the device, the browser and what you were doing when it happened.
