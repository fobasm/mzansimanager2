Mzansi 5th Division

The Mzansi 5th Division is the semi-pro foundation of the pyramid and the starting point for any club you create yourself. Every manager who wants the hard road begins here.

What it is like to manage here

The 5th Division is the most unforgiving place to start, and the most rewarding to escape from. Budgets are small, players are raw, and there is little margin for error. Because new custom clubs always start here, it is also where you will learn the game's systems: how to read a squad, when to buy, and how to build something lasting from almost nothing.

Promotion and relegation

The top two clubs are promoted to the Mzansi 4th Division, and in some seasons a playoff decides the last promotion place. There is no relegation below this division, so the danger is stagnation, not the drop. Every division has twenty clubs and a 38-match league season, with cups and other competitions played around it.

Local rivalries

Several places have more than one club in this division, which creates local rivalries:

  • Kimberley: Kimberley FC, Galeshewe FC

Manager tips for the 5th Division

  • Spend your first window on the weakest position in your starting eleven.
  • Use the youth academy from day one, because it pays back over several seasons.
  • Accept that the first season is about learning and avoiding relegation trouble, not winning everything.
  • Save money for the next window. Good free agents appear at all times of the season.
