The Mzansi League Pyramid

Mzansi Manager is built around a five-division pyramid containing 100 clubs. Every season two clubs rise and two fall between each pair of divisions, so every match matters, from the title race at the top to the survival fights at the bottom.

How a season works

Each division has twenty clubs who play each other home and away, a 38-match season. A win is worth three points, a draw one and a defeat none. The table is decided on points, then goal difference and goals scored. The champions of each division are celebrated at the end of the season, the top two are promoted, and the bottom two are relegated.

Promotion playoffs

In some divisions the final promotion place is decided by a playoff between the clubs finishing just outside the automatic places. The better-placed clubs host the semi-finals and the winners meet in a final. Playoffs add drama to the end of the season, and a strong finish can still win you promotion after a slow start.

Cups and Africa

Alongside the league, clubs compete in the Thatbank Cup, which is open to every club in the country, and the strongest sides also take part in the Mzansi8 and the Bayakhala Knockout. The best clubs qualify for the CAF Champions League. Read more in our cups and African football guide.

Choosing where to start

New managers often pick a familiar club, but the division matters just as much as the badge. A Premier League club offers strong players and big expectations. A lower-division club offers a long climb with more room to build something of your own. If you create your own club, it will start in the Mzansi 5th Division.
