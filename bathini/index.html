Bathini: The Fan Feed Guide

Bathini (isiZulu for "what are they saying?") is the fan feed in Mzansi Manager. It shows how supporters react to your results, your players and your decisions, in the style of a social media timeline.

What Bathini is

The Bathini screen opens with a simple question: what are they saying about you, Coach? You can say something to the fans, or stay quiet. Either way, the fans will talk. The feed fills with posts from fictional supporters, so football in the game feels like football in real life, with praise, jokes and complaints after every match.

Reading a post

Each post shows the fan's name and handle, when it was posted and the message. Many posts are about a specific moment, such as a Man of the Match award. When a player is mentioned, a small card shows his position, age, overall rating and club, so you can see who the fans are talking about without leaving the feed.

Reactions

Under each post are four reaction buttons, each with a count:

  • Talk: join the conversation or see the replies.
  • Spread: share the post so more fans see it.
  • Ayoba: a South African expression of delight, for a post you love.
  • Mxm: the sound of disapproval, for a post you disagree with.

Posts also show how many fans have viewed them and a thread of replies from other supporters. Fans do not always agree, and the replies can turn a good result into an argument.

Why it matters

Bathini is a way to feel how your decisions land. A run of wins brings praise for the players and the manager. A bad result brings criticism, and the replies show which parts of your approach fans are unhappy with. Use the feed to spot a player who is in form, notice a position the fans keep complaining about, and get a sense of the mood around your club before the board reviews your season.

Tips

  • Check Bathini after a big result to see which players the fans are talking about.
  • Use repeated complaints as a hint to look at your lineup or tactics. Our tactics guide can help.
  • Remember that every fan account is fictional. The feed is part of the game and not a real social network.

Frequently asked questions

Are the fans real people?

No. Every account in Bathini is fictional and generated by the game.

Where do I find Bathini?

Look for the Bathini icon in the top bar of the game, next to the mail and search buttons.

Does the name mean anything?

"Bathini" is isiZulu for "what are they saying?", which is the question the feed answers.
