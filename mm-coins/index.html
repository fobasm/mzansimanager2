The MM Coins Guide

MM Coins are a free in-game reward. You earn them by coming back to the game and by completing daily challenges, then convert them into extra transfer budget for your club. This guide explains each part.

What MM Coins are

MM Coins are a small bonus currency that sits next to your club's money. The coin counter is shown in the top bar of the game, beside your transfer budget. Coins are only used inside the game and have no real-world value.

The daily login reward

Open the coins screen once a day and press the claim button to collect that day's coins. The reward runs over a seven-day cycle, and the amounts grow as the streak builds: 15 coins on day 3 and day 4, 20 on day 5, 25 on day 6, and a bonus of 50 coins on day 7. Days you have already claimed are ticked, and today's reward is highlighted.

Daily challenges

Daily challenges give you extra goals to play for while you manage your club. They reset every day, and each one pays coins when you complete it:

  • Win 3 matches in a row: 1 MM Coin.
  • Win 5 matches in a row: 2 MM Coins.
  • Keep 2 clean sheets in a row: 1 MM Coin.
  • Score 4 or more goals in a match: 1 MM
