The MM Coins Guide

MM Coins are a free in-game reward. You earn them by coming back to the game and by completing daily challenges, then convert them into extra transfer budget for your club. This guide explains each part.

What MM Coins are

MM Coins are a small bonus currency that sits next to your club's money. The coin counter is shown in the top bar of the game, beside your transfer budget. Coins are only used inside the game and have no real-world value.

The daily login reward

Open the coins screen once a day and press the claim button to collect that day's coins. The reward runs over a seven-day cycle, and the amounts grow as the streak builds: 15 coins on day 3 and day 4, 20 on day 5, 25 on day 6, and a bonus of 50 coins on day 7. Days you have already claimed are ticked, and today's reward is highlighted.

Daily challenges

Daily challenges give you extra goals to play for while you manage your club. They reset every day, and each one pays coins when you complete it:

  • Win 3 matches in a row: 1 MM Coin.
  • Win 5 matches in a row: 2 MM Coins.
  • Keep 2 clean sheets in a row: 1 MM Coin.
  • Score 4 or more goals in a match: 1 MM Coin.
  • Beat a stronger team: 1 MM Coin.

Each challenge shows your progress, for example 0/3 for a win streak. Streak challenges are easier when your squad is strong and rested, so check stamina and injuries before you start a run of matches.

Converting coins to transfer budget

Under the login reward you will find the converter. Enter how many coins you want to use and press Convert. At your current division, 1 MM Coin is worth R120,000 of transfer budget. Better divisions pay more per coin, so the same coins buy more in the Premier League than in the lower divisions. Conversion is one way: once you have turned coins into budget, you cannot turn the budget back into coins.

How to get the most from your coins

  • Claim the login reward every day so you do not break your streak. The biggest reward comes on day 7.
  • Complete the challenges that match what you are already doing, such as a win streak during a good run of form.
  • Save coins for a division with a better rate if you are close to promotion, since the same coins are worth more there.
  • Use the extra budget on your weakest position. Our transfer market guide explains how to choose.

Frequently asked questions

Do MM Coins cost real money?

No. MM Coins are earned by playing and logging in, and they only have value inside the game.

Can I convert transfer budget back into coins?

No. Conversion is one way.

How often do the challenges reset?

Every day.

Why does a coin buy more in some divisions?

The conversion rate depends on your current division. Better divisions pay more per coin.
