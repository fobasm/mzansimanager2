2D Live Matches

In Mzansi Manager you do not control the players with a controller. You are the manager. You pick the team and the plan, then watch the match play out on a 2D pitch and step in when the game needs you.

Illustration of two sides lined up in a 4-4-2. In the game the players, ball and score update live.

Play a live match

What you see during a match

A live match shows the pitch and the ball, the score and the match clock, a running commentary ticker of key events, and match statistics. The same engine runs every fixture in the pyramid, so the result on your screen is the result that goes into the league table.

What you can do

  • Pause and resume. Stop the match to think and carry on when you are ready.
  • Change tactics. Open the Tactics panel mid-match to change your approach. See below for what each setting does.
  • Make substitutions. You can use up to five substitutions. The Subs button shows how many you have left.
  • Skip to the result. Short on time? Skip to the final score and carry on with the season.
  • Turn live matches off. The Watch matches live setting lets you simulate your matches instantly instead.

Tactics you can set

Each of these changes how many chances you create and concede, and how many cards you are likely to collect.

SettingOptionsWhat it does
MentalityDefensive, Balanced, AttackingDefensive gives fewer chances at both ends and helps an underdog stay in the game. Attacking gives more goals at both ends and pays off for the stronger side.
PressingLow block, Mid block, High pressA low block sits deep and compact with fewer cards. A high press wins the ball high and works best against weaker sides, but players tire after about 60 minutes and cards rise.
TempoSlow, Normal, FastSlow means fewer goals in the match, fast means more. Pace is shared, so it only counts if the other side plays differently.

Your formation matters as well. The game has fourteen, from a classic 4-4-2 to five-at-the-back systems. See formations explained.

Three match situations

The settings make more sense when you see them in use. These are common situations and a sensible response to each.

  • You are the stronger side and the score is level at half-time. Stay patient. An attacking mentality with a mid block or a high press for a spell will create chances, but do not press for ninety minutes. Check the stamina of your midfielders and plan your changes for around the hour mark.
  • You are the underdog and you are drawing. Protect the result. Switch to a defensive mentality and a low block, and keep your shape. A draw against a stronger side is a good result, and defending gives them fewer chances.
  • You are losing late. Change the approach. Move to an attacking mentality with a faster tempo and bring on fresh attackers. This creates more goals at both ends, so it is a risk, but when you need a goal, it is the right one to take.

Using your substitutions

You have five substitutions, and the Subs button shows how many remain. Do not waste them early unless you have a reason. Good reasons are an injury, a player on a booking who might be sent off, and tired legs after a high-pressing spell. The last twenty minutes are when fresh players matter most. Keep one change in reserve in case someone is injured late in the match.

If a key defender has a yellow card and you are protecting a lead, think about replacing him before he picks up a second. The squad rotation guide explains how to manage cards and fatigue across the season.

When to skip to the result

Watching every match is fun, but a 38-match season is long. A good habit is to watch the matches that matter, such as derbies, cup ties and the promotion run-in, and to skip through the others. You can also turn off live matches in the settings and simulate them instantly. The result still goes into the league table in the same way.

Tips for match day

  • If you are the stronger side, an attacking mentality usually pays. If you are the underdog, defend and stay in the game.
  • Do not high-press for ninety minutes. Use it for a spell, or switch to a mid block when legs tire.
  • Use your substitutes. Fresh players matter in the last twenty minutes, and a booking on a key defender is a reason to change.

Frequently asked questions

Do I control the players?

No. You are the manager. You choose the team, formation and tactics, and you make changes during the match. The players act on the pitch according to the game's match engine.

Are watched and skipped matches treated differently?

No. The same engine runs every fixture, so a match you skip or simulate is treated the same way as one you watch.

Can I change my formation during a match?

You can change your tactics from the Tactics panel at any time. Check the formations page for the shapes available in the game.
